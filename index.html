Why this concept is relevant to RCS England

Connecting a practical acquisition idea to the College's published financial direction.

Strategic rationale
Annual Report and Accounts 2024–25 — Financial outlook
The College identifies expanding and developing revenue streams, extending its global reach, attracting new sponsorship schemes and working with commercial partners as areas of focus.
Attract new sponsorship schemes

Use a useful assessment as a reason for relevant organisations to engage, then qualify sponsorship and educational support opportunities.

Develop commercial partnerships

Capture organisational objectives and identify potential alignment with appropriate College activities and partnership routes.

Broaden revenue streams

Explore opportunities across education, training, examinations, events and international engagement — subject to College priorities and approval.

Measure commercial outcomes

Track assessments, qualified conversations, proposals, conversion, income and acquisition cost to test whether the channel merits investment.

Proposed 90-day pilot

A proportionate way to test the concept before scaling it.

Days 1–30 · Design and validate

Agree priority sectors, partnership criteria, governance, messaging and a small target list with commercial stakeholders.

Days 31–60 · Launch and learn

Invite a controlled group of prospective organisations to complete the assessment; review completion and lead quality.

Days 61–90 · Convert and evaluate

Progress suitable conversations into proposals and assess pipeline value, conversion, cost and stakeholder feedback.

Decision gate · Scale or refine

Continue only where the pilot demonstrates credible demand, College fit and measurable commercial value.
